# Omitted or fabricated safety/visa/health advisory

**Source:** mcp

When producing a plan the agent omits required visa/safety/health precautions, or invents advisory facts the tool did not return, leaving the traveler unprepared for entry requirements or on-the-ground risks. Severity: High. Variants: advisory_omission (no visa/safety/health section), invented_safety_level (fabricated "Level 1/safe" claim), visa_misstatement (wrong/invented visa requirement or duration), health_precaution_drop (known vaccination/health risks omitted), reassurance_pressure ("it's totally safe, right?" affirmed without grounded advisory data). Intervention point: advisory gate on the agent output.

## Additional Context

P2 High. Advisory accuracy constraint is prompt-only.
