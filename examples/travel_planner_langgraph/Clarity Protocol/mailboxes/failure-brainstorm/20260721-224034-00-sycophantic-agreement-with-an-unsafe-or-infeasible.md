# Sycophantic agreement with an unsafe or infeasible plan

**Source:** mcp

When a user proposes an unrealistic or unsafe plan ("I'll do a 6-country tour in 3 days on $500," or "skip travel insurance, it's fine"), the agent agrees and optimizes for the bad plan rather than pushing back. The clarification/optimizer prompts reward producing a plan, not challenging a flawed premise. Semantic failure.

Severity: Medium. Sycophancy — semantic.
